**Total images: 112.** Every prompt below is complete and self-contained: the shared art style and "no text" rule are already built into each one. Just click the copy button on the code box, paste into ChatGPT (image generation), and save the result using the ID shown as the filename.

## ⭐ STYLE LOCK (read first) — cinematic & realistic, not cartoon
**Reference look:** a lone standard-bearer in a crimson-and-gold cloak seen from behind, a winged-disc (Faravahar) banner, a vast ancient columned terrace (Persepolis) looming through mist under a heavy storm sky, shallow water and reeds in the foreground. Epic, atmospheric, historically grounded.

**Attach your reference image** (the standard-bearer picture) to the FIRST message of every new ChatGPT chat and say: *"Match the visual style, lighting, colour palette and level of realism of this reference image for every image I ask for. Do not copy its composition."*

**The look in one line:** cinematic photoreal concept art · overcast storm light with breaking sun shafts · volumetric mist · weathered stone & rich fabric · palette of charcoal, storm-grey, sand/ash, **deep crimson** and **burnished antique gold** · shallow depth of field · fine film grain.

**Avoid:** cartoon/animated-film look, bright saturated colours, smooth plastic shading, big-eyed or cute faces, flat vector illustration, text of any kind.

**Kid-safe:** intense and awe-inspiring is good; gore, visible wounds and frightening faces are not. People are shown dignified and respectful, often from behind or at a distance.

**App palette that matches (hex):** night `#0D0C11` · storm `#2A2A31` · ash `#CFC7B8` · crimson `#9B1C24` · burnished gold `#C8A04A`.

## HOW TO GET THE BEST RESULTS

1. **Use one chat for the first 16 core assets**, then start new chats of ~15–20 images each (long chats drift in style).
2. **Do Part C (Characters) before the scenes.** Once you're happy with Shirin, Farhad, Gandom and the Shadow Courier, save the sheets. For any scene that includes a character, attach the character sheet and add this sentence at the start of the prompt: *"Use the attached character sheet exactly for the character design."*
3. **One image per message.** Paste the prompt on its own. If ChatGPT offers two options, pick the one you prefer.
4. **If an image has any stray text or letters:** reply *"Regenerate with absolutely no text, letters or numbers anywhere."*
5. **If a prompt is declined or looks too dark:** soften it, e.g. replace "smoke and embers" with "soft haze and a warm glow", or add *"gentle, child-friendly tone"*.
6. **For cut-out images (white background):** reply *"Now make the background transparent"* or remove it later with Canva, remove.bg or Photoshop. Cut-outs are: logo, coin, seal, trophy, medals, caravan silhouettes, team crests, FX layers.
7. **Seamless textures (CORE-04, CORE-05):** if the edges don't match, reply *"Make this perfectly seamless and tileable."*
8. **Aspect ratios:** ChatGPT's image sizes are roughly 3:2 landscape, 1:1 square and 2:3 portrait; the format is given on each prompt. If it ignores it, say *"Make it landscape."*
9. **Save files with the ID as the name** (e.g. `W03-HERO.png`). The app looks assets up by these IDs.
10. **Prepare for the app:** convert to **WebP** (long edge ≤ 1280 px, ≤ ~120 KB each) with squoosh.app. If the app is published as a Claude Artifact, the builder will embed them as base64 — the smaller the better.
11. **Suggested order:** CORE → TEAM → CHAR → FX → HERO → daily cards week by week → Friday backdrops → Animal cards.

**Optional "chat primer" (paste first in a new chat):**
```
I'm creating artwork for a children's educational adventure app about ancient Persia (ages 9-11, Christian primary school). I'll send you prompts one at a time. For each, generate exactly one image, in a consistent cinematic, photorealistic, moody historical-film style (overcast storm light, mist, muted earthy tones with crimson and burnished-gold accents, fine film grain). Never include any text, letters or numbers in images. Keep everything family-friendly and respectful of Persian/Iranian culture. Ready?
```

**Content reminders:** keep depictions of Persian people and culture respectful and historically inspired (no caricature); no religious figures are drawn in these prompts; battles and fire scenes are symbolic, never graphic.
